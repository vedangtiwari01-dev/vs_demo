STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE - LOAN PROCESSING
Version 1.0 - Effective Date: January 1, 2025

================================================
SECTION 1: MANDATORY WORKFLOW SEQUENCE
================================================

All loan applications MUST follow this exact sequence:

1. Application Received
2. Document Verification
3. Income Verification
4. Credit Check
5. Risk Assessment
6. Manager Approval (for loans > $10,000)
7. Final Approval
8. Disbursement

CRITICAL: Steps must be completed in this order. Skipping steps is prohibited.

================================================
SECTION 2: APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================

Approval Hierarchy:
- Loans up to $10,000: Level 1 Officer approval only
- Loans $10,001 to $50,000: Level 1 Officer + Manager Approval required
- Loans above $50,000: Level 1 Officer + Manager + Senior Manager Approval required

CRITICAL: Self-approval is strictly prohibited. The same officer cannot approve their own processing.

Manager Approval is MANDATORY for all loans exceeding $10,000.

================================================
SECTION 3: TIMING REQUIREMENTS
================================================

Standard TAT (Turn Around Time):
- Document Verification: Must be completed within 24 hours of application
- Credit Check: Must be completed within 48 hours
- Total Processing Time: Minimum 2 hours (to ensure due diligence)
- Total Processing Time: Maximum 7 days from application to disbursement

CRITICAL: Rushed processing (completing entire workflow in less than 2 hours) indicates insufficient due diligence.

================================================
SECTION 4: CREDIT AND ELIGIBILITY
================================================

Credit Score Requirements:
- Minimum credit score: 600
- Loans with credit score below 650 require Risk Assessment sign-off
- Loans with credit score below 600 must be rejected unless Senior Manager exception approval is obtained

Age Eligibility:
- Minimum borrower age: 21 years
- Maximum borrower age: 65 years

================================================
SECTION 5: DOCUMENTATION
================================================

Mandatory Documents:
- Government-issued ID (must be current/not expired)
- Proof of Income (salary slips, bank statements)
- Address Proof
- Credit Report

All documents must be verified before proceeding to Risk Assessment stage.

================================================
SECTION 6: DISBURSEMENT
================================================

Pre-Disbursement Conditions:
- All approvals must be completed
- Final approval must be obtained
- Loan agreement must be signed
- Bank account verification completed

Post-Disbursement QC:
- Quality check must be performed within 48 hours of disbursement
- QC must verify all documents and approvals are in order

================================================
SECTION 7: DATA QUALITY
================================================

Core Fields:
- Case ID, Officer ID, Step Name, Action, Timestamp are mandatory for all workflow entries
- Missing or invalid data in core fields constitutes a critical deviation

Audit Trail:
- All workflow steps must be logged
- Timestamps must be accurate and sequential
