SECTION 1: CUSTOMER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

1.1 AGE ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Age: 21 years at the time of application
- Maximum Age: 65 years at loan maturity

1.2 INCOME REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum Monthly Income for Salaried: INR 25,000 per month

1.3 EMI-TO-INCOME RATIO
- Maximum EMI-to-Income Ratio: 55% of net monthly income for salaried customers

1.4 CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum Credit Score: 650 (CIBIL score)

================================================================================
SECTION 2: APPROVAL AUTHORITY
================================================================================

2.1 APPROVAL LIMITS
- Loans above INR 1,000,000 require Regional Credit Manager approval

================================================================================
SECTION 3: MANDATORY WORKFLOW SEQUENCE
================================================================================

3.1 WORKFLOW STEPS
All applications must follow this sequence:
- Step 1: Application Received and Registration
- Step 2: Customer KYC and AML Verification
- Step 3: Credit Approval

3.2 CRITICAL DEPENDENCIES
- KYC Verification must be completed before Credit Approval

================================================================================
SECTION 4: TIMING REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1 POST-DISBURSEMENT AUDIT
- Post-Disbursement Quality Audit must be completed within 48 hours of disbursement
