LOAN PROCESSING STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Enhanced Features Test Document
Version: 2.0 | Date: January 2025

========================================================================
SECTION 1: CONDITIONAL APPROVAL RULES
========================================================================

1.1 LOAN AMOUNT BASED APPROVALS

Rule 1.1.1: Loans of $10,000 or more MUST require manager approval before final disbursement.

Rule 1.1.2: Loans of $50,000 or more MUST require both manager approval AND senior manager approval.

Rule 1.1.3: Loans below $10,000 may be approved by loan officers directly without manager approval.

========================================================================
SECTION 2: COLLATERAL AND LTV REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================

2.1 LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) RATIO

Rule 2.1.1: The Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio MUST NOT exceed 80% for all secured loans.
Calculation: LTV = Loan Amount Sanctioned / Collateral Value
If LTV > 80%, the loan MUST be rejected OR additional collateral MUST be obtained.

Rule 2.1.2: For gold loans specifically, LTV MUST NOT exceed 75%.

Rule 2.1.3: Collateral value MUST be verified through an independent valuation within the last 6 months.

========================================================================
SECTION 3: PRODUCT-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================

3.1 HOME LOANS

Rule 3.1.1: ALL home loans MUST include a property valuation step completed by a certified valuer.

Rule 3.1.2: Home loans MUST include legal clearance verification for the property title.

Rule 3.1.3: Home loans above $200,000 MUST include property insurance verification.

3.2 GOLD LOANS

Rule 3.2.1: Gold loans MUST include gold purity verification (hallmark check).

Rule 3.2.2: Gold loans do NOT require income verification if loan amount is below $5,000.

3.3 PERSONAL LOANS

Rule 3.3.1: Personal loans MUST include income verification for all amounts.

Rule 3.3.2: Personal loans above $25,000 MUST include credit bureau check.

========================================================================
SECTION 4: CUSTOMER SEGMENT RULES
========================================================================

4.1 PRIORITY CUSTOMERS

Rule 4.1.1: Priority and VIP customers may have EMI-to-Income ratio up to 60% (regular customers: 50% maximum).

Rule 4.1.2: Priority customers may receive expedited processing (24-hour turnaround instead of 72 hours).

4.2 REGULAR CUSTOMERS

Rule 4.2.1: Regular customers MUST maintain EMI-to-Income ratio below 50%.

Rule 4.2.2: Regular customers MUST complete standard 72-hour processing cycle.

========================================================================
SECTION 5: TEMPORAL CONSTRAINTS (TIMING RULES)
========================================================================

5.1 STEP-TO-STEP TIMING REQUIREMENTS

Rule 5.1.1: Manager Approval MUST be completed within 48 hours of Risk Assessment completion.

Rule 5.1.2: Property Valuation (for home loans) MUST be completed within 72 hours of application received.

Rule 5.1.3: Final Disbursement MUST occur within 24 hours of Final Approval.

Rule 5.1.4: Credit Check MUST be completed within 24 hours of Application Received.

5.2 DOCUMENT VALIDITY

Rule 5.2.1: KYC documents MUST be renewed every 12 months. Documents older than 12 months are invalid.

Rule 5.2.2: Salary slips MUST be from the last 3 months. Older salary slips are not acceptable.

========================================================================
SECTION 6: REGULATORY AND PORTFOLIO LIMITS
========================================================================

6.1 CUSTOMER EXPOSURE LIMITS

Rule 6.1.1: Total exposure to a single customer MUST NOT exceed 25% of the bank's total capital.

Rule 6.1.2: Total exposure to a group (related entities) MUST NOT exceed 40% of total capital.

6.2 SECTOR CONCENTRATION

Rule 6.2.1: Total loans to any single sector (Real Estate, Manufacturing, Services, etc.) MUST NOT exceed 15% of the total loan portfolio.

6.3 BRANCH CONCENTRATION

Rule 6.3.1: No single branch should handle more than 30% of the total loan volume to ensure risk distribution.

6.4 SINGLE BORROWER LIMITS

Rule 6.4.1: Individual loan amount MUST NOT exceed 10% of bank's capital for unsecured loans.

========================================================================
SECTION 7: MANDATORY WORKFLOW SEQUENCE
========================================================================

7.1 STANDARD LOAN PROCESSING SEQUENCE

All loans MUST follow this sequence:

1. Application Received
2. Document Verification
3. Credit Check (if applicable per product rules)
4. Income Verification (if applicable per product rules)
5. Risk Assessment
6. Collateral Valuation (for secured loans)
7. Product-Specific Verifications (Property Valuation for Home Loans, Gold Purity for Gold Loans)
8. Manager Approval (if loan amount >= $10,000)
9. Senior Manager Approval (if loan amount >= $50,000)
10. Final Approval
11. Disbursement

========================================================================
SECTION 8: DATA QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
========================================================================

8.1 MANDATORY FIELDS

Rule 8.1.1: The following fields MUST be present in ALL workflow logs:
- case_id (unique loan application identifier)
- officer_id (processing officer)
- step_name (workflow step name)
- action (action taken)
- timestamp (date and time)

Rule 8.1.2: For secured loans, the following additional fields are MANDATORY:
- loan_amount_sanctioned
- collateral_value
- collateral_type

Rule 8.1.3: For home loans, the following fields are MANDATORY:
- product_type = "Home Loan"
- property_value (same as collateral_value)
