SECTION 1: PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) establishes the comprehensive guidelines
and mandatory requirements for processing, approving, disbursing, and managing
Secured Personal Loans. All loan officers, credit managers, operations staff,
and collections personnel shall strictly adhere to the procedures outlined in
this document.

1.2 SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
This SOP applies to:
- Product Type: Secured Personal Loans (collateral-backed)
- Loan Amount Range: INR 100,000 to INR 10,000,000
- Customer Segments: Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals
- Distribution Channels: Branch banking, digital lending platform
- Geographic Coverage: All operational branches and service centers

1.3 REGULATORY FRAMEWORK
This SOP is designed in compliance with applicable banking regulations,
anti-money laundering laws, know-your-customer requirements, and prudential
norms for asset classification and provisioning as prescribed by the regulatory
authority.

1.4 DEFINITIONS AND ACRONYMS
- AML: Anti-Money Laundering
- DPD: Days Past Due
- EDD: Enhanced Due Diligence
- EMI: Equated Monthly Installment
- KYC: Know Your Customer
- LTV: Loan-to-Value Ratio
- NACH: National Automated Clearing House
- NPA: Non-Performing Asset
- PEP: Politically Exposed Person
- QC: Quality Check
- SLA: Service Level Agreement
- TAT: Turn Around Time

SECTION 2: LOAN ORIGINATION WORKFLOW - MANDATORY SEQUENCE
================================================================================

2.1 STANDARD WORKFLOW SEQUENCE
All Secured Personal Loan applications shall follow this mandatory sequence.
Deviations from this sequence are not permitted unless explicitly approved as
an exception.

Step 1: Application Received and Registration
Step 2: Initial Screening and Data Capture
Step 3: Customer KYC and AML Verification
Step 4: Document Collection and Verification
Step 5: Credit Bureau Check
Step 6: Income and Employment Verification
Step 7: Collateral Valuation and Verification
Step 8: Credit Risk Assessment and Scoring
Step 9: Loan Structuring and Pricing
Step 10: Credit Approval (Level 1 - Branch Manager)
Step 11: Credit Approval (Level 2 - Regional Credit Manager) [if required]
Step 12: Credit Committee Approval [if required based on amount/risk]
Step 13: Legal Security Creation and Registration
Step 14: EMI Mandate Setup (NACH/Standing Instruction)
Step 15: Final Pre-Disbursement Quality Check
Step 16: Loan Disbursement
Step 17: Post-Disbursement Documentation
Step 18: Post-Disbursement Quality Audit

2.2 CRITICAL DEPENDENCIES
- KYC Verification (Step 3) shall be completed and approved before proceeding
  to Credit Approval (Steps 10-12)
- Document Verification (Step 4) shall be completed before Credit Approval
- Collateral Valuation (Step 7) shall be completed before Credit Risk
  Assessment (Step 8)
- All Credit Approvals (Steps 10-12) shall be obtained before Legal Security
  Creation (Step 13)
- Legal Security Creation (Step 13) and EMI Mandate Setup (Step 14) shall be
  completed before Loan Disbursement (Step 16)
- Post-Disbursement Quality Audit (Step 18) shall be completed within 48 hours
  of disbursement

2.3 PROHIBITED SEQUENCES
The following sequences are strictly prohibited:
- Disbursement before approval completion
- Disbursement before security creation
- Disbursement before mandate setup
- Credit approval before KYC completion
- Collateral valuation after credit decision
- Duplicate processing of same step for single application

SECTION 3: CUSTOMER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

3.1 AGE ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Age: 21 years (completed) at the time of application
- Maximum Age: 65 years at loan maturity
- Age calculation shall be based on applicant's date of birth as per valid
  government-issued identification document

Exception: For existing premium banking customers with excellent repayment
history (no defaults in last 5 years), maximum age may be extended to 68 years
with Senior Credit Manager approval.

3.2 LOAN TENOR ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Tenor: 12 months
- Maximum Tenor: 84 months (7 years) for standard risk customers
- Maximum Tenor: 60 months (5 years) for higher risk customers (credit score
  between 650-700)
- Tenor shall be structured such that customer's age at maturity does not
  exceed maximum age limit

3.3 INCOME AND EMI-TO-INCOME RATIO
- Minimum Monthly Income:
  * Salaried: INR 25,000 per month
  * Self-Employed: INR 40,000 per month (average of last 12 months)

- EMI-to-Income Ratio (including existing obligations):
  * Maximum: 55% of net monthly income for salaried customers
  * Maximum: 50% of net monthly income for self-employed customers
  * For income above INR 100,000 per month: Maximum 60%

3.4 CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum Credit Score: 650 (CIBIL or equivalent bureau score)
- Preferred Credit Score: 700 and above
- Credit Score Range 650-699: Additional scrutiny required, higher interest
  rate applicable, enhanced documentation required
- Credit Score below 650: Application shall be rejected unless:
  * Customer has substantial collateral (LTV below 50%) AND
  * Credit Committee approval obtained AND
  * Exception documented with clear justification

3.5 EMPLOYMENT AND STABILITY CRITERIA
Salaried Customers:
- Minimum Current Employment: 1 year with current employer
- Total Work Experience: Minimum 2 years
- Employer Type: Government, MNC, listed company, or established private firm

Self-Employed Customers:
- Business Vintage: Minimum 3 years in current business
- ITR Filing: Last 2 years' Income Tax Returns mandatory
- Business Continuity: Same line of business for minimum 3 years

SECTION 4: KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) AND AML COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

4.1 KYC COMPLETION REQUIREMENTS
KYC verification shall be completed and marked as "Verified" or "Approved"
before the application proceeds to any of the following stages:
- Credit approval stages (Branch Manager, Regional Credit Manager, Credit
  Committee)
- Loan disbursement
- Legal documentation execution

4.2 KYC DOCUMENTS - IDENTITY PROOF (One Mandatory)
- Aadhaar Card (preferred)
- Passport
- Voter ID Card
- Driving License
- PAN Card with photograph

4.3 KYC DOCUMENTS - ADDRESS PROOF (One Mandatory)
- Aadhaar Card (preferred)
- Passport
- Utility Bills (electricity, water, gas) - not older than 3 months
- Bank Account Statement - not older than 3 months
- Rent Agreement (registered)

4.4 SANCTIONS SCREENING
All applicants shall be screened against:
- National and international sanctions lists
- Negative list databases
- Adverse media screening

Sanctions Screening Actions:
- If Sanctions Hit = "Yes" or "Confirmed": Application shall be REJECTED
  immediately, no exceptions permitted
- If Sanctions Hit = "Potential" or "Needs Review": Compliance officer review
  required within 24 hours
- Sanctions screening shall be completed before KYC approval status is updated

4.5 POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEP) HANDLING
If applicant is identified as PEP or related to PEP:
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) shall be conducted
- Senior Compliance Officer approval mandatory
- Additional documentation required:
  * Source of income verification (detailed)
  * Source of wealth declaration
  * Business relationships documentation
- PEP applications without EDD completion and compliance approval shall not
  proceed to credit approval

4.6 AML CHECKS
- Transaction monitoring for unusual patterns
- Source of income verification for loan amounts above INR 5,000,000
- High-risk customer identification (cash-intensive business, PEP, adverse
  media)
- Beneficial ownership identification for non-individual applicants

SECTION 5: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

5.1 MANDATORY DOCUMENTS - ALL APPLICANTS
The following documents are mandatory for all Secured Personal Loan applications:

1. KYC Documents:
   - Identity Proof (as per Section 4.2)
   - Address Proof (as per Section 4.3)
   - Photograph (recent, passport size)
   - PAN Card (mandatory for loans above INR 50,000)

2. Income Proof:
   For Salaried:
   - Last 3 months' salary slips
   - Last 6 months' bank statements showing salary credits
   - Form 16 or ITR for last 2 years (for loans above INR 500,000)

   For Self-Employed:
   - Last 2 years' Income Tax Returns with computation
   - Last 12 months' bank statements (business and personal)
   - Balance Sheet and P&L Statement for last 2 years
   - GST Returns for last 12 months (if applicable)

3. Employment/Business Proof:
   For Salaried: Employment Letter or ID Card
   For Self-Employed: Business Registration Certificate, GST Certificate

4. Collateral Documents (detailed in Section 7)

5.2 DOCUMENT VALIDITY REQUIREMENTS
All documents shall be valid and current:
- Identity/Address Proof: Must be valid (not expired)
- Income Proof: Not older than 3 months at the time of application
- Bank Statements: Not older than 1 month at the time of application
- Property Documents: Title clear as per legal verification

Applications with expired documents shall not proceed to approval stage until
valid documents are obtained.

5.3 DIGITAL DOCUMENT SUBMISSION
For applications submitted through digital lending platform:
- All documents shall be uploaded in PDF or image format (JPEG, PNG)
- Document quality: Readable, complete, not tampered
- Self-attested copies acceptable for initial submission
- Original documents verification mandatory before disbursement

5.4 DOCUMENT VERIFICATION CHECKLIST
All documents shall be verified for:
- Authenticity (not forged or altered)
- Completeness (all pages present)
- Validity (not expired)
- Consistency (name, address matches across documents)
- Legibility (readable, clear)

5.5 COLLATERAL DOCUMENTATION (Secured Loans)
Mandatory collateral documents are detailed in Section 7.3. These include:
- Property title documents
- Encumbrance certificate
- Property valuation report
- Property insurance (for property collateral)
- Hypothecation documents (for vehicle/asset collateral)

SECTION 6: CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

6.1 CREDIT BUREAU CHECK
Mandatory credit bureau checks:
- CIBIL Report (mandatory)
- Experian or Equifax (secondary check recommended)
- Bureau report not older than 30 days

6.2 CREDIT RISK GRADING
All applications shall be assigned a risk grade based on comprehensive assessment:

Risk Grade A (Low Risk):
- Credit Score: 750 and above
- Stable employment/business (3+ years)
- No adverse credit history
- LTV below 70%
- EMI-to-Income ratio below 45%

Risk Grade B (Medium Risk):
- Credit Score: 700-749
- Stable employment/business (2+ years)
- Minor credit delays (1-2 instances of 30 DPD in last 2 years)
- LTV 70-75%
- EMI-to-Income ratio 45-55%

Risk Grade C (Higher Risk):
- Credit Score: 650-699
- Acceptable employment/business stability
- Credit delays within acceptable limits (up to 60 DPD, max 2 instances)
- LTV 75-80%
- EMI-to-Income ratio up to 55%

Risk Grade D (Unacceptable Risk):
- Credit Score: Below 650
- Frequent credit delays (90+ DPD or 3+ instances of 60 DPD)
- Multiple loan rejections
- High existing obligations
- Application shall be rejected (Exception: Credit Committee approval with
  strong collateral)

6.3 EXISTING OBLIGATIONS VERIFICATION
- Verification of existing loans from bureau report
- Calculation of total monthly obligations
- Assessment of repayment capacity after accounting existing loans
- Cross-verification with bank statements

6.4 FRAUD CHECKS
- Negative area list verification
- Negative database check (internal defaulter list)
- Employment/business verification through third-party agencies
- Site visit for loan amounts above INR 2,000,000

SECTION 7: COLLATERAL AND SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

7.1 ACCEPTED COLLATERAL TYPES
The following collateral types are accepted for Secured Personal Loans:

Primary Collateral:
1. Residential Property (self-occupied or rented)
2. Commercial Property
3. Fixed Deposits with the bank
4. Gold (minimum 22 carat)
5. Securities (stocks, bonds, mutual funds)

Secondary Collateral (co-collateral):
6. Vehicle (car, commercial vehicle)
7. Machinery and Equipment

7.2 LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) RATIO LIMITS
Maximum LTV ratios for different collateral types:

Residential Property:
- Self-occupied: Maximum 75%
- Rented property: Maximum 70%
- Under-construction: Not accepted

Commercial Property:
- Office space: Maximum 70%
- Shop/Retail: Maximum 65%

Fixed Deposits:
- Bank's own FD: Maximum 90%
- Other bank FD: Maximum 80%

Gold:
- Maximum 75% of current market value

Securities:
- Equity shares: Maximum 50%
- Mutual funds: Maximum 60%
- Government securities: Maximum 80%

Vehicle:
- Car (own use): Maximum 60%
- Commercial vehicle: Maximum 50%

7.3 COLLATERAL VALUATION REQUIREMENTS
Collateral valuation shall meet the following requirements:

Property Valuation:
- Valuation by bank-empaneled valuer (mandatory)
- Valuation report not older than 90 days at the time of disbursement
- Site visit mandatory
- Valuation report shall include: Property description, location details,
  market value assessment, distress sale value, legal status
- For loan amounts above INR 5,000,000: Two independent valuations required

Asset/Vehicle Valuation:
- Current market value as per recognized valuation guides
- Physical verification mandatory
- Insurance valuation certificate

Securities Valuation:
- Current market price (last traded price)
- Average of last 30 days for volatile securities
- Haircut as per LTV limits

7.4 VALUATION VALIDITY AND REFRESH
- Property valuation: Valid for 90 days from valuation date
- If disbursement delayed beyond 90 days: Fresh valuation required
- Re-valuation mandatory for any modification in loan amount

7.5 LEGAL SECURITY CREATION
Legal security shall be created and registered before loan disbursement:

For Property Collateral:
- Mortgage deed execution
- Registration with sub-registrar office
- Original property documents deposited with bank
- Encumbrance certificate obtained (post-mortgage)

For Gold:
- Physical possession taken by bank
- Gold stored in bank locker/strong room
- Insurance coverage obtained

For Securities:
- Pledge created in bank's favor
- Demat account lien marked
- Pledge confirmation obtained

For Fixed Deposits:
- Lien marked on FD
- Duplicate FD receipt issued in bank's favor

For Vehicles:
- Hypothecation registered with RTO
- RC book endorsed with bank's hypothecation
- Original RC book deposited with bank

7.6 COLLATERAL INSURANCE
Collateral insurance is mandatory:
- Property: Fire and special perils insurance
- Vehicle: Comprehensive motor insurance
- Coverage amount: Minimum equal to loan amount
- Bank to be named as loss payee/beneficiary
- Insurance policy validity to cover entire loan tenor

SECTION 8: APPROVAL AUTHORITY MATRIX
================================================================================

8.1 APPROVAL LEVELS BASED ON LOAN AMOUNT
Loan approval authority is assigned based on loan amount and risk grade:

BRANCH MANAGER (Level 1):
- Loan Amount: Up to INR 1,000,000
- Risk Grade: A or B only
- Additional Conditions: LTV below 70%, Credit Score 700+

REGIONAL CREDIT MANAGER (Level 2):
- Loan Amount: INR 1,000,001 to INR 3,000,000
- Risk Grade: A, B, or C
- Mandatory for all Risk Grade C applications
- Mandatory for LTV above 75%

CREDIT COMMITTEE (Level 3):
- Loan Amount: Above INR 3,000,000
- Any loan amount with Risk Grade C
- Any loan with LTV above 75%
- Any loan requiring policy exception
- Composition: Credit Head, Risk Head, Operations Head (minimum 3 members)

8.2 RISK-BASED APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
Additional approval requirements based on risk factors:

Credit Score 650-699:
- Regional Credit Manager approval mandatory (regardless of amount)
- Enhanced documentation and verification
- Higher interest rate loading

EMI-to-Income Ratio above 50%:
- Regional Credit Manager approval mandatory
- Detailed income stability assessment

Negative Area/High-Risk Location:
- Regional Credit Manager approval mandatory
- Enhanced due diligence
- Physical site visit mandatory

8.3 EXCEPTION APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
Exceptions to this SOP require explicit approval:

Minor Exceptions (Branch Manager may approve):
- Age up to 66 years (instead of 65)
- Employment tenure 11 months (instead of 12)
- Document delay by 1-2 days

Major Exceptions (Credit Committee approval mandatory):
- Credit score below 650
- LTV above 80%
- Age above 66 years
- Tenor beyond maximum limit
- EMI-to-Income ratio above 60%

All exceptions shall be documented with:
- Reason for exception
- Compensating factors
- Approver name and designation
- Date of approval

8.4 APPROVAL DECISION VALIDITY
Loan approval is valid for:
- Standard cases: 90 days from approval date
- If not disbursed within validity: Fresh approval required
- Approver may extend validity by 30 days with documented reason

SECTION 9: DISBURSEMENT PROCEDURES
================================================================================

9.1 PRE-DISBURSEMENT CONDITIONS - MANDATORY CHECKLIST
Loan disbursement shall occur ONLY after ALL of the following conditions are met:

1. Credit approval obtained from appropriate authority (as per Section 8)
2. KYC verification completed and approved
3. All mandatory documents collected and verified
4. Legal security created and registered (as per Section 7.5)
5. EMI mandate/NACH set up and confirmed as "Active" or "Registered"
6. Collateral valuation completed (not older than 90 days)
7. Property insurance obtained (for property collateral)
8. Loan agreement executed by customer
9. Pre-disbursement quality check completed and signed off

Any disbursement without meeting all pre-conditions is a critical deviation and
subject to strict disciplinary action.

9.2 EMI MANDATE SETUP REQUIREMENT
EMI mandate setup is mandatory before disbursement:
- NACH (National Automated Clearing House) mandate preferred
- Standing instruction with customer's salary/business account
- Mandate amount: Minimum equal to EMI amount + 10% buffer
- Mandate status shall be "Registered", "Active", or "Set" before disbursement
- Mandate confirmation from bank's clearing system

Exception: For Fixed Deposit backed loans, EMI mandate may be set up as interest
adjustment from FD (no external mandate required).

9.3 DISBURSEMENT AMOUNT ACCURACY
Disbursed amount shall match sanctioned amount:
- Tolerance: ±0.5% of sanctioned amount permitted
- Any variance beyond ±0.5% requires fresh approval
- Disbursement amount cannot exceed sanctioned amount under any circumstances
- If customer requests lower disbursement: Acceptable with written request

9.4 DISBURSEMENT MODE
Loan amount shall be disbursed through:
- Direct credit to customer's bank account (preferred)
- Pay order/Demand draft (for specific payment requirements)
- RTGS/NEFT transfer

Cash disbursement is strictly prohibited for amounts above INR 50,000.

9.5 DISBURSEMENT ACCOUNT VERIFICATION
Before disbursement:
- Customer's account details verified
- Account holder name matches loan applicant name
- Account active and operational
- Penny drop test conducted (for digital channel disbursements)

9.6 POST-DISBURSEMENT QUALITY CHECK
Post-disbursement quality audit is mandatory:
- Timeline: Within 48 hours of disbursement
- Conducted by: Operations Quality Team (independent from origination team)
- Checklist verification:
  * All pre-disbursement conditions met
  * Documents complete and properly filed
  * Legal security registration confirmed
  * Mandate setup verified
  * Disbursement amount correctness
  * System data entry accuracy

- Quality check status shall be marked as "Completed" or "Passed"
- Any adverse finding: Reported to Credit Manager within 24 hours

9.7 POST-DISBURSEMENT DOCUMENTATION
Within 7 days of disbursement:
- Loan agreement copy provided to customer
- Repayment schedule provided to customer
- Welcome letter sent
- Collateral documents filed properly
- Digital loan file updated with all documents

SECTION 10: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS (SLA) AND TURNAROUND TIME
================================================================================

10.1 BUSINESS HOURS DEFINITION
Standard business hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Weekends and public holidays: Non-working days (SLA calculation excludes these)

10.2 STEP-WISE TAT REQUIREMENTS
Each workflow step shall be completed within specified TAT:

Application Registration: Same day (within 4 hours)
Initial Screening: Same day (within 4 hours)
KYC Verification: 1 working day
Document Collection: 2 working days (dependent on customer)
Credit Bureau Check: Same day (within 2 hours)
Income Verification: 1 working day
Collateral Valuation: 3-5 working days (external valuer dependent)
Credit Risk Assessment: 1 working day
Credit Approval (Branch Manager): Same day (within 4 hours of complete file)
Credit Approval (Regional Manager): 1 working day
Credit Committee Approval: 2 working days
Legal Security Creation: 5-7 working days (registration dependent)
Mandate Setup: 1-2 working days (NACH registration dependent)
Pre-Disbursement QC: Same day (within 4 hours)
Disbursement: Same day (within 2 hours of QC clearance)

10.3 OVERALL TURNAROUND TIME (TAT)
End-to-end loan processing TAT (from application to disbursement):
- Standard cases: 10-12 working days
- Complex cases (high value, multiple approvals): 15-18 working days
- Express processing (for existing premium customers): 5-7 working days

10.4 SLA BREACH HANDLING
TAT breach consequences:
- Minor breach (1-2 days delay): Escalation to supervisor
- Major breach (3+ days delay): Escalation to Regional Manager
- Chronic breaches: Performance review and corrective action

10.5 TIMING RESTRICTIONS
Certain critical activities have timing restrictions:
- Credit approval shall not be given on the same day as application submission
  (minimum 24 hours gap for due diligence)
- Disbursement shall not occur on the same day as credit approval (minimum
  12 hours gap for security creation)
- Post-disbursement QC shall be completed within 48 hours (not later)

Maximum gap between steps:
- If any step pending for more than 15 days: Application marked as "stale"
- Stale applications require customer re-confirmation and document refresh

SECTION 11: COLLECTIONS, RECOVERY, AND RESTRUCTURING
================================================================================

11.1 COLLECTION ESCALATION SCHEDULE
Collection actions shall be initiated based on Days Past Due (DPD):

0-15 DPD (Bucket 0):
- Automated SMS/email reminders on due date and +3 days
- Courtesy call by customer service (Day 7)
- No adverse action

16-30 DPD (Bucket 1):
- Daily SMS/email reminders
- Collection officer call (Day 16, 20, 25)
- Payment arrangement discussion
- Late payment charges applied

31-60 DPD (Bucket 2):
- First Reminder Notice sent (physical + email) on Day 31
- Collection officer field visit (if amount > INR 500,000)
- Escalation to Senior Collection Manager
- Payment plan negotiation
- Account status: "Special Mention Account"

61-90 DPD (Bucket 3):
- Final Reminder Notice sent on Day 61
- Legal Notice issued on Day 75
- Guarantor/co-borrower contacted
- Field visit mandatory
- Account status: "Special Mention Account - SMA 2"

91-180 DPD (NPA - Substandard):
- Account classified as Non-Performing Asset (NPA)
- Legal action initiated (suit filing process started) on Day 100
- SARFAESI notice issued (if applicable)
- Asset recovery process initiated
- Account status: "Substandard Asset"

181-365 DPD (NPA - Doubtful):
- Continued legal proceedings
- Asset auction/sale proceedings
- Settlement negotiations
- Account status: "Doubtful Asset"

366+ DPD (NPA - Loss):
- Write-off consideration
- Final settlement attempts
- Account status: "Loss Asset"

11.2 COLLECTION ESCALATION COMPLIANCE
Failure to escalate as per schedule is a serious deviation:
- Collection actions shall not be delayed beyond scheduled timelines
- All escalation steps shall be documented in system
- Physical proof of notice delivery required

11.3 LOAN RESTRUCTURING REQUIREMENTS
Loan restructuring is permitted under specific circumstances:

Restructuring Eligibility:
- Genuine financial hardship (job loss, business disruption, medical emergency)
- No willful default
- Restructuring request before account becomes NPA (before 90 DPD)
- Customer demonstrates repayment intent

Restructuring Approval Authority:
- Loan Amount up to INR 1,000,000: Regional Credit Manager
- Loan Amount above INR 1,000,000: Credit Committee
- Any NPA account restructuring: Credit Committee + CEO approval

Restructuring shall NOT be granted without proper approval authority.
Unauthorized restructuring is a critical compliance violation.

Restructuring Documentation:
- Customer's restructuring request (written)
- Financial hardship evidence
- Revised repayment schedule
- Approval from appropriate authority
- Updated loan agreement

11.4 WRITE-OFF REQUIREMENTS
Loan write-off is permitted only under strict conditions:

Write-off Eligibility:
- Account in "Loss" category (365+ DPD)
- All recovery efforts exhausted
- Legal proceedings concluded or unviable
- Write-off recommended by Collections Head

Write-off Approval Authority:
- All write-offs: Board of Directors approval mandatory
- Write-off proposal preparation: Chief Credit Officer
- Credit Committee recommendation required before Board approval

Write-off shall NOT occur without Board approval. Any unauthorized write-off
will result in strict disciplinary action and potential fraud investigation.

Write-off Documentation:
- Detailed write-off proposal
- Recovery effort summary
- Legal opinion
- Board meeting minutes
- Regulatory reporting compliance

SECTION 12: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND ASSET CLASSIFICATION
================================================================================

12.1 ASSET CLASSIFICATION FRAMEWORK
All loan accounts shall be classified based on Days Past Due (DPD) as follows:

STANDARD ASSET:
- DPD Range: 0 to 29 days
- Definition: Loan account with no overdue or overdue up to 29 days
- Provisioning Requirement: 0.25% of outstanding amount
- Reporting: Regular loan portfolio

SPECIAL MENTION ACCOUNT - SMA 0:
- DPD Range: 0 (current but showing signs of stress)
- Early warning indicators tracked
- Provisioning: 0.25%

SPECIAL MENTION ACCOUNT - SMA 1:
- DPD Range: 30-60 days
- Provisioning: 0.25%
- Enhanced monitoring required

SPECIAL MENTION ACCOUNT - SMA 2:
- DPD Range: 61-89 days
- Provisioning: 0.50%
- Pre-NPA status, intensive collection efforts

SUBSTANDARD ASSET (NPA):
- DPD Range: 90 to 365 days
- Definition: Non-Performing Asset for less than or equal to 12 months
- Provisioning Requirement: 15% of outstanding amount
- NPA Classification: Mandatory upon crossing 90 days
- Interest Accrual: Stopped (interest recognized on cash basis only)

DOUBTFUL ASSET (NPA):
- DPD Range: 366 to 730 days (12-24 months)
- Definition: NPA for more than 12 months but less than 24 months
- Provisioning Requirement: 25% of outstanding amount (unsecured portion)
- Secured portion provisioning: 15% (if realizable value of security sufficient)

LOSS ASSET (NPA):
- DPD Range: 731+ days (24+ months)
- Definition: NPA for more than 24 months
- Provisioning Requirement: 100% of outstanding amount
- Write-off recommendation mandatory

12.2 ASSET CLASSIFICATION COMPLIANCE
Asset classification shall be accurate and timely:
- Classification review: Monthly (as on month-end date)
- System update: Within 7 days of month-end
- Classification based on actual DPD (not projected DPD)
- Any misclassification (reporting lower DPD category than actual) is a serious
  regulatory violation

12.3 PROVISIONING REQUIREMENTS
Provisioning shall be made as per classification:
- Provisioning calculated on outstanding principal + accrued interest
- Provisioning adequacy verified monthly
- Any provisioning shortfall (actual provisioning less than required) shall be
  immediately rectified

Provisioning Shortfall Tolerance:
- Acceptable tolerance: ±2% of required provisioning
- Beyond 2% shortfall: Reported as regulatory deviation
- Shortfall rectification: Within same month

12.4 NPA IDENTIFICATION AND REPORTING
NPA identification process:
- Daily monitoring of accounts approaching 90 DPD
- System alert at 85 DPD for pending accounts
- Automatic NPA marking on 90th day
- NPA classification cannot be delayed or suppressed

NPA Reporting:
- Monthly NPA report to senior management
- Quarterly NPA report to Board of Directors
- Regulatory reporting as per prescribed format and timeline
- Any delay in NPA reporting or misclassification will be treated as serious
  compliance failure

12.5 REGULATORY REPORTING SCHEDULE
- Monthly: Portfolio quality report, NPA movement, provisioning status
- Quarterly: Detailed asset quality review, restructuring report
- Annual: Asset classification audit, credit policy compliance report

Missing or late regulatory reports for NPA accounts (90+ DPD) will be flagged
as critical compliance deviation.

SECTION 13: PORTFOLIO LIMITS AND CONCENTRATION RISK
================================================================================

13.1 CUSTOMER EXPOSURE LIMITS
Maximum exposure to single customer (including group entities):

Individual Customer:
- Single Secured Personal Loan: INR 10,000,000 (maximum)
- Total Exposure (all loan products): INR 15,000,000 (maximum)
- If customer requests additional loan exceeding limit: Credit Committee
  approval with special justification

Group Exposure (related parties):
- Total Group Exposure: INR 25,000,000 (maximum)
- Related party definition: Family members, business partners, common directors

Any loan sanctioned exceeding customer exposure limit without proper approval
is a serious deviation.

13.2 SECTOR CONCENTRATION LIMITS
Maximum portfolio concentration in specific sectors:

Real Estate Sector:
- Maximum: 20% of total Secured Personal Loan portfolio
- Includes loans taken for real estate purchase, construction, or where
  borrower's primary income is from real estate business

NBFC/Financial Services Sector:
- Maximum: 15% of total portfolio
- Includes borrowers employed in or owning NBFC/financial services firms

Hospitality and Tourism Sector:
- Maximum: 10% of total portfolio

Textile and Garment Sector:
- Maximum: 8% of total portfolio

Emerging/High-Risk Sectors:
- Maximum: 5% of total portfolio per sector

Sector concentration review: Monthly
Exceeding sector limit: New loan sanctions in that sector require Credit
Committee approval with concentration risk assessment.

13.3 BRANCH CONCENTRATION LIMITS
Maximum portfolio concentration at branch level:

Single Branch Exposure:
- Maximum: 15% of total Secured Personal Loan portfolio
- Objective: Prevent geographic concentration risk

High-Performing Branch:
- May be permitted up to 20% with Regional Manager approval
- Requires monthly monitoring

New Branch:
- First 12 months: Maximum 5% of portfolio
- Ensures quality over quantity in new branches

13.4 GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION LIMITS
State/Region Level Concentration:
- Single State: Maximum 30% of total portfolio
- Single Metro City: Maximum 25% of total portfolio
- Single Tier-2 City: Maximum 10% of total portfolio

Objective: Diversification to mitigate regional economic risks

13.5 CONCENTRATION RISK MONITORING
- Monthly portfolio concentration analysis
- Alert triggers when concentration reaches 80% of limit
- Sanctions restricted when limit reached (Credit Committee override possible)
- Annual concentration risk stress testing

SECTION 14: DATA QUALITY AND AUDIT TRAIL STANDARDS
================================================================================

14.1 MANDATORY DATA FIELDS
The following fields are mandatory for every loan application and shall not be
left blank or null:

Core Mandatory Fields:
- case_id (unique application reference number)
- officer_id (loan officer/processor ID)
- step_name (current workflow step name)
- action (action taken: approved, rejected, pending, etc.)
- timestamp (date and time of action)

Customer Information:
- customer_name
- customer_age
- customer_mobile
- customer_email
- customer_address

Loan Details:
- loan_amount_requested
- loan_amount_sanctioned (after approval)
- tenor_months
- interest_rate
- emi_amount

Any application with missing core fields shall not proceed to next stage.

14.2 DATA FORMAT STANDARDS
Data shall be entered in prescribed formats:

Date Format: YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2025-01-14)
Time Format: HH:MM:SS (24-hour format)
Currency Format: Numeric only, no currency symbols (e.g., 500000, not INR 5 Lakhs)
Phone Number Format: 10 digits (e.g., 9876543210)
Email Format: Valid email with @ and domain
PAN Format: 10 characters (AAAAA9999A)
Aadhaar Format: 12 digits

Invalid format entries shall be rejected by system validation.

14.3 DATA CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENTS
Data consistency across workflow steps:
- Customer name shall remain consistent across all steps (no variation)
- Loan amount shall not change without approval modification
- Customer age, PAN, Aadhaar shall be immutable (cannot change once entered)
- Any inconsistency in core data across steps is a data quality deviation

14.4 DUPLICATE CASE DETECTION
System shall prevent duplicate active applications:
- Duplicate detection based on: PAN, Aadhaar, Mobile Number, Email
- If duplicate active case found: New application shall be rejected or merged
- Same customer can have multiple loans only if previous loan is closed or in
  repayment with good track record

14.5 AUDIT TRAIL REQUIREMENTS
Complete audit trail mandatory for all applications:
- Every action logged with timestamp, user ID, and action taken
- No deletion of historical records permitted
- Modification history tracked (what changed, when, by whom)
- Audit log retention: Minimum 7 years

Missing audit trail or log gaps will be flagged as serious compliance issue.

14.6 DATA QUALITY AUDIT
Monthly data quality audit:
- Random sample of 5% of loans reviewed for data accuracy
- Completeness check (no missing mandatory fields)
- Consistency check (data matches across documents)
- Format compliance check

Data quality score maintained per branch and per loan officer.

SECTION 15: CONDITIONAL AND PRODUCT-SPECIFIC RULES
================================================================================

15.1 PRODUCT VARIANTS
This SOP covers standard Secured Personal Loans. Product variants may have
modified rules as specified:

15.1.1 SECURED PERSONAL LOAN - SALARIED (Standard Product)
All rules in this SOP apply as stated.

15.1.2 SECURED PERSONAL LOAN - SELF-EMPLOYED (Product Variant)
Modified rules:
- Minimum income: INR 40,000/month (higher than salaried)
- Business vintage: Minimum 3 years
- ITR mandatory: Last 2 years
- EMI-to-Income: Maximum 50% (lower than salaried)
- Additional documents: GST returns, Business bank statements 12 months
- Valuation: Mandatory for all loan amounts (no waiver)

15.1.3 SECURED PERSONAL LOAN - GOLD BACKED (Product Variant)
Modified rules:
- Maximum loan amount: INR 5,000,000
- Maximum LTV: 75% of gold value
- Gold purity: Minimum 22 carat
- Gold valuation: By certified gold assessor
- Physical possession: Mandatory (gold stored in bank locker)
- Maximum tenor: 48 months (shorter than standard)
- Simplified documentation: Income proof may be waived for loans < INR 1,000,000

15.1.4 SECURED PERSONAL LOAN - PROPERTY BACKED (Product Variant)
Modified rules:
- Property valuation: Mandatory (no waiver)
- Legal opinion: Mandatory from bank's panel advocate
- Property insurance: Mandatory throughout loan tenor
- Mortgage registration: Mandatory before disbursement
- Maximum LTV: 75% for residential, 70% for commercial
- Property age: Maximum 30 years for residential, 20 years for commercial

15.2 CUSTOMER SEGMENT-SPECIFIC RULES

15.2.1 EXISTING CUSTOMERS (Premium Banking)
Existing customers with excellent relationship (no defaults, premium banking
relationship of 5+ years) may receive:
- Expedited processing (5-7 days TAT)
- Maximum age relaxation: 68 years (instead of 65)
- Pre-approved loan offers (subject to refreshed credit check)
- Documentation: Some income documents may be waived (bank statements sufficient)
- Interest rate: Preferential rate (0.5% lower than standard)

15.2.2 NEW CUSTOMERS
Standard rules apply. No relaxations permitted for first-time customers.

15.2.3 HIGH NET WORTH INDIVIDUALS (HNI)
Customers with net worth > INR 10,000,000 and relationship value > INR 5,000,000:
- Dedicated relationship manager
- Customized loan structures permitted (with Credit Committee approval)
- Higher loan amounts: Up to INR 15,000,000 (instead of INR 10,000,000)
- EMI-to-Income: May be relaxed to 60% (with adequate collateral)

15.3 CHANNEL-SPECIFIC RULES

15.3.1 BRANCH CHANNEL (Traditional)
- All documentation in physical form acceptable
- Original document verification mandatory
- Personal interaction with customer required
- TAT: 10-12 working days (standard)

15.3.2 DIGITAL LENDING PLATFORM (Online)
- Digital documentation acceptable (PDF, scanned images)
- eKYC permitted (Aadhaar-based)
- Video KYC acceptable (for identity verification)
- eSign for loan agreement
- TAT: 5-7 working days (faster processing)
- Loan amount limit: INR 3,000,000 (lower than branch channel)
- Face-to-face verification waived for loans < INR 1,000,000 with eKYC

15.4 LOAN AMOUNT-BASED RULES

15.4.1 SMALL TICKET LOANS (INR 100,000 to INR 500,000)
Simplified processing:
- Branch Manager approval sufficient (no escalation)
- Valuation: Simplified (desktop valuation acceptable)
- Credit Committee: Not required
- Income verification: Bank statements of 3 months sufficient
- Processing time: 5-7 days

15.4.2 MEDIUM TICKET LOANS (INR 500,001 to INR 3,000,000)
Standard processing:
- All rules as per this SOP apply
- Regional Credit Manager approval required for amounts > INR 1,000,000
- Full valuation mandatory
- Processing time: 10-12 days

15.4.3 LARGE TICKET LOANS (INR 3,000,001 to INR 10,000,000)
Enhanced scrutiny:
- Credit Committee approval mandatory
- Two independent valuations required
- Enhanced documentation: Wealth statement, source of income declaration
- Field investigation mandatory
- Senior management interview (loan amounts > INR 5,000,000)
- Processing time: 15-18 days

15.5 EXCEPTION SCENARIO RULES
Certain scenarios require conditional approvals:

Scenario: Credit Score 650-699 (Below preferred but above minimum)
Condition: Regional Credit Manager approval + Enhanced documentation + Higher
interest rate (+1% loading)

Scenario: Customer Age 63-65 years
Condition: Tenor restricted to ensure maturity before age 68 + Life insurance
cover mandatory

Scenario: Self-employed with business vintage 2-3 years (less than standard 3 years)
Condition: Higher collateral (LTV max 65%) + Co-borrower with stable income +
Credit Committee approval

Scenario: LTV 75-80% (higher than standard 75%)
Condition: Excellent credit score (750+) + Stable employment (5+ years) +
Regional Credit Manager approval

Scenario: EMI-to-Income 55-60% (higher than standard 55%)
Condition: Very high income (>INR 100,000/month) + Stable employment + Additional
collateral security
