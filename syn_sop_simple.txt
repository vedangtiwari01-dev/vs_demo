SECTION 1: MANDATORY WORKFLOW SEQUENCE
================================================================================

All Secured Personal Loan applications must follow this mandatory sequence:

Step 1: Application Received and Registration
Step 2: Initial Screening and Data Capture
Step 3: Customer KYC and AML Verification
Step 4: Document Collection and Verification
Step 5: Credit Bureau Check
Step 6: Income and Employment Verification
Step 7: Collateral Valuation and Verification
Step 8: Credit Risk Assessment and Scoring
Step 9: Credit Approval (Level 1 - Branch Manager)
Step 10: Credit Approval (Level 2 - Regional Credit Manager) [if loan amount > INR 1,000,000]
Step 11: Legal Security Creation and Registration
Step 12: EMI Mandate Setup
Step 13: Final Pre-Disbursement Quality Check
Step 14: Loan Disbursement
Step 15: Post-Disbursement Quality Audit

CRITICAL DEPENDENCIES:
- KYC Verification (Step 3) must be completed before Credit Approval (Steps 9-10)
- Document Verification (Step 4) must be completed before Credit Approval
- Collateral Valuation (Step 7) must be completed before Credit Risk Assessment (Step 8)
- All Credit Approvals must be obtained before Legal Security Creation (Step 11)
- Legal Security Creation and EMI Mandate Setup must be completed before Disbursement

PROHIBITED SEQUENCES:
- Disbursement before approval completion is strictly prohibited
- Credit approval before KYC completion is not allowed

SECTION 2: CUSTOMER ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

2.1 AGE ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Age: 21 years (completed) at the time of application
- Maximum Age: 65 years at loan maturity

Exception: For existing premium customers with excellent repayment history, maximum
age may be extended to 68 years with Senior Credit Manager approval.

2.2 LOAN TENOR ELIGIBILITY
- Minimum Tenor: 12 months
- Maximum Tenor: 84 months (7 years) for standard risk customers
- Maximum Tenor: 60 months (5 years) for higher risk customers with credit score
  between 650-700

2.3 INCOME REQUIREMENTS
Minimum Monthly Income:
- Salaried Customers: INR 25,000 per month
- Self-Employed Customers: INR 40,000 per month (average of last 12 months)

2.4 EMI-TO-INCOME RATIO
Maximum EMI-to-Income Ratio (including existing loan obligations):
- Salaried Customers: 55% of net monthly income
- Self-Employed Customers: 50% of net monthly income
- High Income Customers (income > INR 100,000/month): 60% allowed

2.5 CREDIT SCORE REQUIREMENTS
- Minimum Credit Score: 650 (CIBIL or equivalent bureau score)
- Preferred Credit Score: 700 and above
- Credit Score below 650: Application rejected unless substantial collateral (LTV < 50%)
  AND Credit Committee approval obtained

2.6 EMPLOYMENT STABILITY
Salaried Customers:
- Minimum Current Employment: 1 year with current employer
- Total Work Experience: Minimum 2 years

Self-Employed Customers:
- Business Vintage: Minimum 3 years in current business
- ITR Filing: Last 2 years Income Tax Returns mandatory

================================================================================
SECTION 3: KYC AND AML COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

3.1 KYC COMPLETION REQUIREMENT
KYC verification must be completed and marked as "Verified" before the application
proceeds to Credit Approval or Loan Disbursement.

3.2 KYC DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
Identity Proof (one mandatory):
- Aadhaar Card (preferred)
- Passport
- Driving License
- PAN Card with photograph

Address Proof (one mandatory):
- Aadhaar Card (preferred)
- Utility Bills (not older than 3 months)
- Bank Account Statement (not older than 3 months)

3.3 SANCTIONS SCREENING
All applicants must be screened against national and international sanctions lists.

Actions:
- If Sanctions Hit = "Yes": Application REJECTED immediately, no exceptions
- Sanctions screening must be completed before KYC approval

3.4 POLITICALLY EXPOSED PERSONS (PEP)
If applicant is identified as PEP:
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) mandatory
- Senior Compliance Officer approval mandatory
- Additional source of income and wealth verification required
- PEP applications without EDD and compliance approval cannot proceed to credit approval

================================================================================
SECTION 4: DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

4.1 MANDATORY DOCUMENTS FOR ALL APPLICANTS

Income Proof:
For Salaried:
- Last 3 months salary slips
- Last 6 months bank statements showing salary credits
- Form 16 or ITR for last 2 years (for loans above INR 500,000)

For Self-Employed:
- Last 2 years Income Tax Returns with computation
- Last 12 months bank statements (business and personal)
- Balance Sheet and P&L Statement for last 2 years

4.2 DOCUMENT VALIDITY
- Income Proof: Not older than 3 months at application time
- Bank Statements: Not older than 1 month at application time
- Identity/Address Proof: Must be valid (not expired)

Applications with expired documents cannot proceed to approval stage until valid
documents are obtained.

4.3 COLLATERAL DOCUMENTATION
For Property Collateral:
- Property title documents mandatory
- Encumbrance certificate mandatory
- Property valuation report mandatory

For secured loans with property collateral, all property documents must be complete
before disbursement.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: CREDIT RISK ASSESSMENT
================================================================================

5.1 CREDIT BUREAU CHECK
- CIBIL Report mandatory for credit bureau check
- Bureau report must not be older than 30 days

5.2 CREDIT RISK GRADING
Risk Grade A (Low Risk):
- Credit Score: 750 and above
- Stable employment (3+ years)
- LTV below 70%
- EMI-to-Income ratio below 45%

Risk Grade C (Higher Risk):
- Credit Score: 650-699
- Acceptable employment stability
- LTV 75-80%
- EMI-to-Income ratio up to 55%

Risk Grade D (Unacceptable Risk):
- Credit Score: Below 650
- Application rejected (Exception: Credit Committee approval with strong collateral)

================================================================================
SECTION 6: COLLATERAL AND SECURITY
================================================================================

6.1 LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) RATIO LIMITS

Residential Property:
- Self-occupied property: Maximum LTV 75%
- Rented property: Maximum LTV 70%

Commercial Property:
- Office space: Maximum LTV 70%
- Shop/Retail: Maximum LTV 65%

Gold:
- Maximum LTV 75% of current market value

Fixed Deposits:
- Bank's own FD: Maximum LTV 90%

6.2 COLLATERAL VALUATION
Property Valuation Requirements:
- Valuation by bank-empaneled valuer mandatory
- Valuation report must not be older than 90 days at disbursement time
- Site visit mandatory
- For loan amounts above INR 5,000,000: Two independent valuations required

6.3 VALUATION VALIDITY
- Property valuation: Valid for 90 days from valuation date
- Valuation older than 90 days: Fresh valuation required before disbursement

Applications with stale valuations (older than 90 days) cannot proceed to disbursement.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: APPROVAL AUTHORITY AND HIERARCHY
================================================================================

7.1 APPROVAL LIMITS BY LOAN AMOUNT

Branch Manager (Level 1):
- Loan Amount: Up to INR 500,000
- Risk Grade: A and B only

Regional Credit Manager (Level 2):
- Loan Amount: INR 500,001 to INR 3,000,000
- Risk Grade: A, B, and C

Credit Committee:
- Loan Amount: Above INR 3,000,000
- All risk grades including exceptions

7.2 APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
- Loans above INR 500,000: Regional Credit Manager approval mandatory
- Loans above INR 1,000,000: Regional Credit Manager approval mandatory
- Loans above INR 3,000,000: Credit Committee approval mandatory

7.3 SELF-APPROVAL PROHIBITION
- Same officer cannot originate AND approve the same loan application
- Self-approval detected: Application flagged as serious process violation

================================================================================
SECTION 8: DISBURSEMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

8.1 PRE-DISBURSEMENT CONDITIONS
Before disbursement, the following must be completed:
- All credit approvals obtained
- Legal security creation completed
- EMI mandate setup completed (NACH/Standing Instruction)
- Pre-disbursement quality check completed

8.2 MANDATE SETUP REQUIREMENT
- Repayment mandate must be set up before disbursement
- Mandate status must be "Active" or "Registered"
- Disbursement without active mandate is prohibited

8.3 DISBURSEMENT VERIFICATION
- Disbursed amount must equal sanctioned loan amount
- Disbursement to loan account or directly to beneficiary as per approval
- Disbursement approval and execution on same business day

================================================================================
SECTION 9: POST-DISBURSEMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

9.1 POST-DISBURSEMENT QUALITY AUDIT
- Post-Disbursement Quality Audit must be completed within 48 hours of disbursement
- Audit verifies: Documentation completeness, approval compliance, disbursement accuracy
- Cases with audit findings: Remedial action within 5 business days

9.2 POST-DISBURSEMENT DOCUMENTATION
- Loan agreement executed and signed
- Security documents registered (for property collateral)
- Insurance coverage activated (for property/vehicle collateral)

================================================================================
SECTION 10: TIMING AND TAT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

10.1 STEP TIMING CONSTRAINTS

Credit Bureau Check to Credit Approval:
- Maximum Time: 72 hours
- Breach: Escalation to Regional Credit Manager

Credit Approval to Disbursement:
- Maximum Time: 7 business days
- Breach: Senior Management notification

10.2 CRITICAL TIMING RULES
- Post-Disbursement Quality Audit must be completed within 48 hours of disbursement
- KYC Verification to Credit Approval: Maximum 5 business days
- Document Collection to Verification: Maximum 3 business days

================================================================================
SECTION 11: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

11.1 CUSTOMER EXPOSURE LIMITS
- Maximum exposure to single customer (including group entities): INR 15,000,000
- This is 25% of the bank's capital base of INR 60,000,000
- Exposure exceeding limit: Board approval mandatory

11.2 AUDIT TRAIL REQUIREMENTS
Complete audit trail mandatory for all applications:
- Every action logged with timestamp, user ID, and action taken
- Missing audit trail: Flagged as serious compliance issue

11.3 DATA QUALITY REQUIREMENTS
Core fields mandatory for every workflow log entry:
- case_id: Unique loan application identifier
- officer_id: Officer handling the case
- step_name: Workflow step name
- action: Action taken (approved, rejected, completed)
- timestamp: Date and time of action

Missing core fields: Data quality violation, case flagged for review

================================================================================
SECTION 12: COLLECTIONS AND DELINQUENCY
================================================================================

12.1 OVERDUE MANAGEMENT
Days Past Due (DPD) Classification:
- 1-30 DPD: Bucket 1 - Soft reminder calls
- 31-60 DPD: Bucket 2 - Field visit mandatory
- 61-90 DPD: Bucket 3 - Legal notice issued

12.2 COLLECTION ESCALATION
- Cases reaching 31+ DPD: Escalation to Collections Manager mandatory
- Escalation delay beyond 5 days: Process violation
